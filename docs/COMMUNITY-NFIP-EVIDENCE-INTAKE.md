# Community NFIP Evidence Intake

Use this form only to collect evidence needed for a historical NFIP/FIRM reconciliation. Participation is voluntary and privacy-minimized.

## Historical flood-map record
For each policy/map period: policy year; FEMA FIRM panel/suffix; FIRM effective date; flood zone; BFE value, units and vertical datum; source document ID.

## Insurance record
For each policy year: redacted policy identifier (optional); annual premium; building/contents coverage; building/contents deductibles; fees/surcharges; rating methodology shown in the record; documented correction/refund/adjustment; supporting evidence ID.

## Elevation evidence
Record elevation value, units, vertical datum, survey/control information, date, professional certification, and supporting evidence ID.

## Flood/structure history
Record known flood claims/documented flooding, flood-zone changes, observed nearby infrastructure changes, and supporting photographs/maps/documents.

## Privacy
Do **not** submit Social Security numbers, passwords, bank-account information, payment-card data, or unnecessary personally identifying information. Redact unneeded policy identifiers.

All records are evidence inputs, not proof of misrating, map error, causation, or damages.
