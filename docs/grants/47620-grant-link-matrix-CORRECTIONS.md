## Verified programs and entry points

Federal portals: Grants.gov `https://www.grants.gov/` · SAM.gov/UEI
`https://sam.gov/content/home`

FEMA mitigation: HMGP `https://www.fema.gov/grants/mitigation/hazard-mitigation`
(post-disaster only) · **BRIC — CLOSED, no FY26 NOFO** ·
**FMA — CLOSED, no FY26 NOFO**

USACE (correct district): Louisville District `https://www.lrl.usace.army.mil/`
— entry point for Planning Assistance to States, Flood Plain Management
Services, and **Section 205** small-flood-projects authority. Note: USACE CAP
Section 205 requires a **public sponsor**; an individual cannot self-sponsor.
Section 204 beneficial use requires the non-federal sponsor to pay 35% of the
incremental cost — **no in-kind credit under Section 204**.

Indiana: IFA CWSRF `https://www.in.gov/ifa/clean-water-state-revolving-fund/` ·
IFA DWSRF `https://www.in.gov/ifa/drinking-water-state-revolving-fund/` ·
IFA Brownfields `https://www.in.gov/ifa/brownfields/` ·
IDEM §319 `https://www.in.gov/idem/nps/nonpoint-source-section-319-grants/` ·
OCRA CDBG `https://www.in.gov/ocra/cdbg/` (stormwater subprogram
`https://www.in.gov/ocra/cdbg/stormwater-management-program/`) ·
**DNR LARE** — most relevant state flood program, typically due Jan 15 ·
**Indiana Flood Control Revolving Fund (IC 14-28-5)** — real, administered by
IFA since 2015, loans to local units for flood-control works including dikes
and levees. Only the county (as the local unit) can apply.

USDA RD: Community Facilities
`https://www.rd.usda.gov/programs-services/community-facilities/community-facilities-direct-loan-grant-program`

Law enforcement/school safety (if ever relevant): DOJ SVPP
`https://cops.usdoj.gov/svpp` · COPS Hiring `https://cops.usdoj.gov/chp` ·
Indiana CJI (Byrne JAG state admin) `https://www.in.gov/icji/`
