## Hypothesis gate
**Question:** Did any historical/current hydraulic-control assumption associated with navigation infrastructure materially affect the FEMA flood-hazard analysis applicable to the study area, and can that effect be reproduced from primary records and quantified against observed physical evidence?

Required evidence: infrastructure existence/geometry; construction/modification/abandonment chronology; operating rules or defensible historical assumptions; proof of dependency in the relevant FEMA model; reproducible inputs/boundary conditions; quantified hydraulic effect; and comparison with independent observations.

Historical scholarship is a research lead, not proof of present FEMA model dependency.

## Insurance reconciliation gate

Risk Rating 2.0 is contextual methodology evidence only where applicability is documented; a lower modeled premium is not by itself proof of prior misrating.
