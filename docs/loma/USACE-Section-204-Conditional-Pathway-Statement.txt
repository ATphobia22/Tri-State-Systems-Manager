1. CONDITIONAL NATURE OF STRUCTURAL FILL IDENTIFICATION
This statement establishes the regulatory framework for integrating dredged
materials derived from regional USACE river channel maintenance operations.
In strict alignment with the governing parameters of the TSM architecture,
dredged materials are NOT presumed to be structural fill.

USACE Section 204 is processed exclusively as a CONDITIONAL AUTHORITY PATHWAY.
This record does not imply automatic infrastructure funding, nor does it guarantee
the localized engineering properties or availability of material.

2. SPECIFIC MATERIAL QUALIFICATION CRITERIA
Before any sediment or dredged composite is cleared to enter the engineering
evidence ledger for structural deployment (such as emergency access corridor
stabilization or zero-rise road elevation), it must pass the following multi-tier
qualification gates:
  A. Geotechnical Suitability: Laboratory verification of shear strength, USCS
     classification profile, and high-water saturation behavior (Bishop FoS >= 1.20).
  B. Environmental Review: Strict compliance validation against NEPA benchmarks and
     Indiana Department of Environmental Management (IDEM) clean-fill standards.
  C. Project-Specific Verification: Independent evaluation of economic cost-sharing,
     beneficial-use parameters, and formal sign-off by the USACE Louisville District.

3. RELATION TO CONCURRENT FEMA LOMA FILINGS
This conditional statement is attached to the FEMA Letter of Map Amendment (LOMA)
evidence packet to formalize the boundary between natural terrain clearance profiles
(Lowest Adjacent Grade at 377.2 ft NAVD88 vs. Base Flood Elevation at 375.0 ft NAVD88)
and future potential structural fill operations. The confirmation of natural ground
clearance stands independently of any prospective Section 204 beneficial use
agreements, ensuring that regulatory floodway constraints remain unaffected by
unmaterialized infrastructure material options.
