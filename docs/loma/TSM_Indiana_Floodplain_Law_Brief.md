# Indiana Floodplain Law — Operating Brief (TSM)

**Audience:** Local submittals, LOMA support packages, county FPA coordination  
**Not legal advice.** Confirm current text of IC 14-28 and 312 IAC 10 before relying.

## 1. Core statutes and rules

| Source | Role |
|--------|------|
| **IC 14-28-1** | Flood control; IDNR Division of Water licensing in floodways |
| **IC 14-28-3** | Local floodplain management / community ordinances |
| **312 IAC 10** | Minimum state standards for flood plain delineation and regulation |

**Purpose of 312 IAC 10** (paraphrase of published scope): decrease existing flood damages, mitigate future damages, promote health/safety/welfare; keep floodways free of undue capacity restrictions. Flood plains subject to regulation generally along waterways with drainage area **≥ 1 square mile** (with stated exceptions).

## 2. Who does what

| Actor | Function |
|-------|----------|
| **FEMA** | NFIP maps (FIRM/NFHL), LOMC (LOMA/LOMR), mandatory purchase rules |
| **IDNR Division of Water** | State floodway licenses; Best Available Floodplain (BAFL); INFIP / FARA tools; ordinance approval standards |
| **Local floodplain administrator (FPA)** | Enforce community flood damage prevention ordinance; permits in SFHA; coordinate with IDNR/FEMA |
| **Property owner** | Applications, certified elevations where required, LOMA filings |

Posey County public contact used in TSM packages: County Floodplain Administrator (Jerry Cotner — jerry.cotner@poseycountyin.gov). Verify current official of record before formal submission.

## 3. Floodway vs fringe vs LOMA

- **Floodway:** Channel + portion of floodplain needed to pass base flood without increasing water surface beyond regulatory criteria. Construction in floodway often needs **IDNR license** under IC 14-28-1 in addition to local permits.
- **Fringe:** Remainder of floodplain outside floodway; local ordinance rules typically dominate for structures.
- **LOMA (FEMA):** Property/structure may be removed from SFHA if natural ground (or structure) is above BFE per FEMA process — **does not by itself** waive state floodway licensing if the site is still in a state-regulated floodway.

## 4. INFIP and FARA (Indiana practice)

The **Indiana Floodplain Information Portal (INFIP)** provides FEMA and DNR floodplain information and can generate a **Floodplain Analysis and Regulatory Assessment (FARA)**.

Published DNR guidance indicates FARA is used for local permitting and for LOMA support in **Zone A**, and for regulatory determination when upstream drainage area > 1 sq mi, unmapped areas, or known flood-prone sites. Users may contest FARA results through the portal’s review path.

TSM may **assist** assembly of coordinates, elevations, and documentation; it does **not** replace FARA, IDNR review, or FEMA LOMC decisions.

## 5. Local ordinance duty

Communities in the NFIP adopt and enforce a flood damage prevention ordinance no less effective than 312 IAC 10 (for ordinances after the dates stated in the rule). Local FPAs issue permits, require elevation documentation, and work with IDNR/FEMA on map issues.
