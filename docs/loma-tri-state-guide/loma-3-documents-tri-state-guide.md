# How to Obtain the Three LOMA "Still Needed" Document Types Across the Tri-State River Valley

**Coverage:** 8 counties — Posey, Vanderburgh, Warrick (IN); Henderson, Union (KY); White, Wabash, Gallatin (IL)
**Researched:** 2026-10-23 via web search. URLs come only from search results; anything not found is stated honestly.
**Note:** FEMA LOMA MT-1 applications require: (1) a recorded deed showing ownership, (2) a tax assessor map / plat of the property, and (3) a FEMA Elevation Certificate (Form 086-0-33) or equivalent survey completed by a licensed professional land surveyor or professional engineer.

## Document Type 3 — PE/RLS-Certified Elevation

FEMA LOMA (MT-1) elevation data must come from a **FEMA Elevation Certificate (Form 086-0-33)** completed by a **licensed professional land surveyor (PLS/RLS) or licensed professional engineer (PE)**. Steps: (1) verify the professional's license with the state board below, (2) have them perform a field survey to NAVD88 datum and complete the Elevation Certificate, (3) attach the signed/sealed EC to the MT-1.

### Indiana — verify a PE or surveyor license
- **Indiana Professional Licensing Agency (PLA), MyLicense lookup:** mylicense.in.gov — public lookup by name, license number, profession, city, county, or status (supports partial-name wildcard search).
- **Contacts:** State Board of Registration for Professional Engineers and State Board of Registration for Professional Surveyors — both c/o PLA, email pla10@pla.in.gov, 402 W. Washington St., Room W072, Indianapolis, IN 46204, (317) 234-3022. PLA contact page: https://secure.in.gov/pla/contact-pla/
- **Surveyor directory:** Indiana Society of Professional Land Surveyors "Find A Surveyor" — https://ispls.org/find-a-surveyor/

### Kentucky — verify a PE or land surveyor license
- **Kentucky Board of Licensure for Professional Engineers and Land Surveyors (KBELS), Searchable Roster:** https://kyboels.ky.gov/Consumer-Information/Pages/default.aspx — search by license type or number, licensee name, status, and county. Board site: kyboels.ky.gov (160 Democrat Drive, Frankfort, KY 40601).
- **Kentucky surveyor association directory:** NOT FOUND this session — no KAPS "find a surveyor" directory URL was captured.

### Illinois — verify a PE or land surveyor license
- **Illinois Dept. of Financial and Professional Regulation (IDFPR), License Lookup:** https://onlinedfpr.micropact.com/lookup/licenselookup.aspx — public search of all IL professional license holders (PE and Professional Land Surveyor).
- **PLS program page:** https://idfpr.illinois.gov/profs/LandSurv.html (also covers the CORE online licensing system).
- **Surveyor directory:** Illinois Professional Land Surveyors Association company directory — https://careers.iplsa.org/directory/?ca=all
- **National directory (all states):** https://rpls.com/surveyors/ — "Find a Surveyor" browseable by state and by service (including elevation certificates).

### Practical tips
- Ask for the Elevation Certificate (FEMA Form 086-0-33), not just a "survey letter." Confirm the surveyor/engineer references **NAVD88 datum** and the FEMA FIRM panel for the property.
- The county surveyor's office (where one exists) is a government office — it cannot recommend a private surveyor; use the directories above.
- For tri-state-border properties, the surveyor must be licensed in the state where the property sits.
