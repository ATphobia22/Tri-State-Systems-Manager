# Community Engineering Scope

TSM is a community-scale decision-support and evidence system for the lower Wabash–Ohio River Valley.

It does not identify or prioritize a private residence. Public engineering layers operate on river reaches, transportation corridors, farms, utilities, public/community assets, floodplain units, watersheds, and project-defined areas of interest.

Historical family observations may be preserved as generalized `HISTORICAL_COMMUNITY_EVIDENCE` with provenance and privacy controls.

Engineering output is review evidence, not professional certification or agency acceptance.
