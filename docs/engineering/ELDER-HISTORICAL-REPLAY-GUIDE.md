# Historical Replay Guide

Historical maps, plats and community records are evidence of past conditions. They are not automatically current cadastral, regulatory or engineering truth.

## Replay rules

1. Preserve source date and provenance class.
2. Render historical geometry in a distinct historical layer.
3. Compare historical and current sources without overwriting either source.
4. Record transformations and derived measurements.
5. Treat uncertain georeferencing as provisional.
6. Require human review before using historical material as engineering evidence.

Historical replay helps communities understand landscape change, infrastructure history and watershed context while preserving the distinction between historical evidence and current authoritative products.
