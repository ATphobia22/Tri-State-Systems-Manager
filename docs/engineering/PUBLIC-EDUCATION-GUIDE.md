# Public Education Guide

TSM presents engineering and environmental information as decision support.

## What the public can rely on

- Source agencies remain the authority for published observations and regulatory products.
- Live observations are labeled with source and retrieval state.
- Stale or unavailable sources are not silently replaced with guessed values.
- Historical layers are labeled historical and do not overwrite current regulatory or cadastral truth.
- Simulations and visualizations are labeled as derived or demonstrative.

## What TSM does not do

TSM does not issue floodplain permits, LOMA/LOMR/CLOMR decisions, No-Rise certifications, levee accreditation, professional survey certifications or agency acceptance.

## Reading map layers

FEMA effective flood mapping and Indiana Best Available Floodplain Mapping are separate layers. Terrain visualization may be exaggerated for readability; that display setting does not change engineering elevation values.

## Safety

Public visualization is not an emergency-management instruction and is not a substitute for current official warnings, evacuation instructions or professional engineering advice.
