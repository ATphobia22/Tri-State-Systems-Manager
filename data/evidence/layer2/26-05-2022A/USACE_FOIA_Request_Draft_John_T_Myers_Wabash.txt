Dear FOIA Officer:

Pursuant to the Freedom of Information Act, 5 U.S.C. § 552, and applicable
Department of the Army / USACE implementing regulations, I request copies of
the following records. This request is made in connection with historical and
technical research concerning federal navigation and water-resources works in
the Tri-State River Valley (Ohio–Wabash confluence region) and does not
presuppose any conclusion about present FEMA flood-model dependency.

I. SCOPE — JOHN T. MYERS LOCKS AND DAM (formerly Uniontown Locks and Dam)

1. Authorization and purpose documents establishing the project as a
   replacement for Locks and Dam 48 and 49, including references to
   Section 6 of the Rivers and Harbors Act of March 3, 1909, as amended,
   and the September 17, 1958 Secretary of the Army authorization.

2. Project purpose statements distinguishing navigation functions from
   flood-control or multipurpose reservoir storage (including any
   statements that pool storage is navigational only).

3. Design memoranda, general design memoranda, or equivalent describing
   the navigation pool, lock chambers (1,200 ft and 600 ft), dam length,
   tainter gates, and design lift.

4. Construction history summaries: locks construction start/completion
   (reported circa 1965–1969) and dam construction start/completion
   (reported circa 1970–1977).

5. Any operations manuals or water-control plans describing normal
   pool regulation for navigation versus high-flow / open-river
   conditions.

6. Records relating to the Water Resources Development Act of 2000
   authorization for lock improvement / auxiliary-chamber extension,
   if available without excessive search.

II. SCOPE — WABASH / NEW HARMONY NAVIGATION AND BANK WORKS (HISTORICAL)

7. Historical records, reports, or maps concerning federal navigation
   works, cut-offs, dams, dikes, dredging, or bank stabilization in the
   New Harmony / Grand Rapids reach of the Wabash River (19th and 20th
   century), to the extent reasonably segregable and still maintained
   by the Louisville District or successor records repositories.

8. Any “308 Report” or basin studies for the Wabash that discuss
   levees, reservoirs, or local protection concepts in Posey County
   or the lower Wabash, if still held by the District.

III. SCOPE — FLOOD-RELATED LOCAL PROTECTION (CONTEXT ONLY)

9. Indexes or lists of completed, inactive, or deauthorized local
   flood-protection or levee projects in Posey County, Indiana, or
   immediately adjacent reaches of the lower Wabash and Ohio, sufficient
   to identify project names and authorization status. Full design
   drawings are not required at this stage.

IV. FORMAT AND LIMITATIONS

- Prefer electronic copies (PDF or native digital) where available.
- If volumes are large, please contact me to discuss prioritization
  or rolling releases before incurring substantial search/review costs.
- I request a fee waiver or fee estimate in advance if fees will exceed
  $50. This request is for non-commercial research and public-interest
  documentation of federal water-resources history in the Tri-State
  River Valley.
- Please exclude purely personal privacy information and classified
  material; segregate and release all reasonably segregable portions.

V. STATEMENT OF NON-CONCLUSION

This request seeks primary records. It does not assert that John T. Myers
Locks and Dam was authorized or operated as a 100-year flood-control
project, nor that any present FEMA flood insurance rate map or model
depends improperly on these works. Those questions, if pursued, will be
evaluated only after primary records are obtained.
