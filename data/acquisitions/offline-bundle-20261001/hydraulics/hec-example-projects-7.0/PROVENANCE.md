## Coverage note (do not over-claim)
These are USACE **training/example** projects, not a model of the Wabash–Ohio
confluence reach. The most relevant Indiana content is the **Muncie** example
(`2D Unsteady Flow Hydraulics/Muncie/` — White River reach, real Indiana data,
ships with 1D and 2D variants). No example models the confluence reach itself.

## What remains unavailable (see MISSING.md)

  USACE publication channels (verified 2026-10-01).

- The Ohio River Community HEC-RAS Model (operational, NWS OHRFC) is not
