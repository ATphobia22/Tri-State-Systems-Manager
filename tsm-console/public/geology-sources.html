    How to use these sources

    Open the official item record to inspect the publisher, original document and rights. For terrain or engineering analysis, obtain a separately licensed georeferenced source, verify its footprint, CRS, geoid, units and accuracy, and obtain independent site control. A historical map title is not proof it covers a particular parcel.

    For present-day soils, consult USDA NRCS. For modern elevation, consult USGS The National Map. Neither dataset is created from these historical citations.
